Sales and gross profit are read from the Talon income statement. Margin, the projection and the comparison columns are the only calculated values on this page.

What changes
  • Month to date is no longer a tab. Its columns are always on screen.
  • Tabs become Day w/ MTD (default), vs. Last Month, vs. Last Year.
  • Day w/ MTD adds two columns: the day's sales and gross profit.
  • Comparison tabs add four: the prior month's sales, gross profit, margin, and % of that month.
  • Projected month-end is a new group, on Day w/ MTD only.
Projected month-end Projected = MTD ÷ open days elapsed × total open days — here MTD ÷ 9 × 26, factor 2.89.
  • Sales and gross profit only. No projected margin — both scale by the same factor, so it would equal the month-to-date margin on every row.
  • Day w/ MTD tab only.
  • Two day counts, deliberate. Do not reconcile them. Header, progress bar and comparison colour use the accounting period, 10 of 30. The projection uses a six-day trading week, 9 of 26, which understates month-end on purpose.
  • The column group must state the projection basis — · 26 open days — so the two counts are not read as an error.
Comparison columns
  • Prior columns are a complete month — all of August, all of September 2025 — not the same 10 days of it. The export only reports finished periods.
  • % of prior = MTD gross profit ÷ that month's gross profit.
  • Colour against 33.3% elapsed: green at +3 points or more, red at −3 or worse, otherwise plain.
  • Day w/ MTD columns are values, not comparisons — never coloured.
