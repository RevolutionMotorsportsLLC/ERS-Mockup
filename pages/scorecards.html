Scorecards

Month to date only, for now
  • The old tool let you switch the period (Day, MTD, and others). This pass ships month to date only — a single day is too noisy for a fair cross-store comparison at a smaller store (one deal either way swings every ratio), and half the stores would show mostly dashes. MTD gives every store a real number on every column. Day is the natural next pass, for whoever wants to check today specifically.
  • Every store shown here is one of the seven already used elsewhere in this mockup. The live preview of this page also lists an eighth, Pensacola — worth confirming with Logan whether that's a real store or stale sample data before it goes further.
