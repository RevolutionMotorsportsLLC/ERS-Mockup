Motorclothes Summary

Every revenue channel, and the totals built from them.

A variation on Parts Sales, not a new design
  • Same conversion as Parts: the legacy page's four period tabs and separate Sales/Profits toggle collapse into the standard three-tab pattern, since one table already shows dollars, gross profit and margin together.
  • Motorclothes genuinely has a simpler channel mix than Parts — no Cust. Pay – Service, Warranty or Deal Add-On in this store's data, not a trimmed-down copy of Parts' row list. The live page already reflects this: those rows don't appear on its Motorclothes Summary either.
  • No Day column on this table — accurate day-of data was more complexity than the near-term scope could absorb. MTD is the default tab, not Day w/ MTD. The Counter Sales table below is the exception and keeps its Day figures, since each row there is a rep's day, not a comparison basis on a store-wide metric.
Projected month-end Projected = MTD ÷ open days elapsed × total open days
  • Applied to every dollar row. No projected margin — both figures scale by the same factor, so it would equal the month-to-date margin on every row. MTD tab only.
  • Uses ERS's own six-day trading week, which differs on purpose from the live page's Projected column (straight calendar days elapsed) — same divergence as Bike Sales, Store Overview and Parts Sales.
Comparison columns
  • Prior column is a complete month — all of August, all of September 2025 — not the same 11 days of it.
  • % of prior = MTD ÷ that month's figure. Gross Margin shows a difference instead. Where a channel is genuinely zero in both periods (Employee, Wholesale), the ratio is undefined and shown as a dash, not 0.0%.

Counter Sales

Today's sales and month-to-date productivity, by rep.

Reduced from 17 columns to 7
  • Fewer than Parts' 11: Motorclothes has no Cust. Pay, Warranty, Deal Add-On or Internal activity for any rep in this store's data, and Employee / Wholesale are store-wide, not rep-attributed. Counter Sales is the only channel any rep actually has, so it stands alone as the day figure rather than being summed into a separate "Day Sales" column that would just repeat it.
  • Avg Ticket and LIPT are derived (MTD counter sales ÷ tickets, and items ÷ tickets) — not stored, computed from the counts to the left.
  • Unlike Parts, this export's five reps already sum to the Summary table's totals above — no sample rows were needed to make this table reconcile.

Build Notes

Changes against the live page.

What's different from the current Motorclothes page
  • Changed: four period tabs and a Sales/Profits toggle collapsed into the standard three-tab pattern, matching Parts Sales and every other tab in ERS.
  • Changed: the Summary table's Day column and Day w/ MTD tab are gone — MTD is the default tab now. The Counter Sales table below keeps its Day figures, since each row there is a rep's day.
  • Changed: the per-rep table drops every column that's always zero for Motorclothes (Cust. Pay, Warranty, Deal Add-On, Internal, Employee, Wholesale), keeping only what actually varies by rep.
  • Removed: Inventory (bin cycle counts) and Obsolescence. The live page's version of these sections is byte-identical to what's on the Parts Sales page — same bins, same $117,540 inventory figure, same 12.1% / 5.9% obsolescence — because it's store-wide inventory data, not specific to Motorclothes. Showing the same numbers under two different tabs invites someone to wonder whether that's intentional or a copy-paste bug; the answer lives on Parts Sales once, not here twice.
  • Removed for now: cross-store ranking on this tab. That's what Scorecards' Motorclothes table is for.

Source bands. The tag beside each row label says where the figure comes from: statement read from the Talon income statement or POS export, counted from unit or ticket records, derived calculated by ERS.
