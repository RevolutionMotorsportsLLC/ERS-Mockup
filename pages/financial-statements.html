Balance Sheet — build notes
  • Full line-item fidelity, deliberately — every account Harley's schema defines, not a curated summary. This is a document to rely on, not a dashboard.
  • Fixed Assets alone has three amount columns (Cost, Accum. Deprec., Net Value) instead of one — the real statement's own layout, kept as-is rather than flattened.
  • Access: gate this statement — cash, debt and equity lines especially — to owner/GM roles through ERS's existing permission tooling. Don't build new logic; wire into what exists.

Income Statement — build notes
  • No time tabs, no MTD, no Projected month-end — the deliberate exception to the Time tabs convention elsewhere in ERS. A statement only exists for a closed month; no forecast belongs on a document submitted as-is. Current + complete Year to Date, always.
  • This tab runs one closed month behind the rest of ERS, on purpose — every other tab shows today's in-progress month; this shows the most recently closed one. Not a bug — flag to Logan whether a live in-progress view is ever wanted too.
  • Every subtotal in the Operating Summary chain is arithmetic on statement figures, same as the Balance Sheet's section totals — tagged statement, not derived. Margin and the two %-of columns are the only genuinely calculated figures on this tab.
