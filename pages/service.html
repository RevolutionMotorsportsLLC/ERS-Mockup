Service Summary

Labor, parts and sublet, plus what the shop earns per RO.

Per RO and % of Total, folded in rather than a separate panel
  • The live page shows these as two small side panels next to the Closed ROs table, one for the day and one for the month. They're arithmetic on numbers already in this Summary table (Labor/Parts/Sales ÷ Total Close), so they're rows here instead — consistent with how every other KPI table in ERS keeps its derived ratios in one place rather than a separate widget.
  • No live page had a Gross Profit or Margin row for Service, so none is invented here — Labor and Parts don't have a cost basis in this export the way Bike Sales and Parts department sales do.
  • No Day column on this table — accurate day-of data was more complexity than the near-term scope could absorb. MTD is the default tab, not Day w/ MTD. The Closed ROs table below is the exception and keeps its Day scope, since each row there is an advisor's day, not a comparison basis on a store-wide metric.
Projected month-end Projected = MTD ÷ open days elapsed × total open days
  • Applied to Labor, Parts, Sublet, Total Sales and Total Close. No projected Per RO or % of Total — both halves of each ratio scale by the same factor, so it would equal the month-to-date figure on every row. MTD tab only.
  • Uses ERS's own six-day trading week, which differs on purpose from the live page's Projected column (straight calendar days elapsed) — same divergence as every other tab in ERS.
Comparison columns
  • Prior column is a complete month — all of August, all of September 2025 — not the same 11 days of it.
  • % of prior = MTD ÷ that month's figure on Labor, Parts, Sublet and Total Sales. Per RO and % of Total show a difference instead, same treatment as Gross Margin elsewhere in ERS.

One column dropped, one kept on request
  • Int Close is zero for every advisor, in both the day and month tables — dropped as carrying no information. Cust Close and Warr Close are also zero for every advisor here, but stay: unlike Int Close, no one asked to drop them, and a store with different payer-close habits might use them.
  • Total Close is its own reported figure, not Cust Close + Warr Close + Int Close — those three sum to zero in this export while Total Close doesn't (Hughes closed 8 ROs today with all three count columns at zero). Worth flagging for whoever writes the real formula spec: summing the close-type columns would silently produce the wrong number.
  • Total Labor & Parts is kept as its own column despite being Total Parts + Total Labor added together — kept on request rather than trimmed as redundant.
  • Where Total Close is zero, % Parts and Avg Ticket are undefined and shown as a dash, not 0% or $0 — no ROs closed means no ratio to report, not a ratio of zero.
