Bike Sales KPIs

Fifteen figures, five rows across New, Used and New + Used.

Segment columns, not segment blocks
  • New, Used and New + Used were fifteen stacked rows (five metrics × three segments). They're three column groups now, five rows total — New against Used reads on one line, which is how a sales manager compares the floor.
  • No Day column, on this table or the Transactions table below — getting accurate day-of data reliably was more complexity than the near-term scope of the project can absorb. MTD is the default tab now, not Day w/ MTD. Record-level tables (Per Deal, below) are the exception and keep theirs.
Projected month-end Projected = MTD ÷ open days elapsed × total open days — here MTD ÷ 7 × 26, factor 3.71.
  • Applied to units, sales and gross, per segment — labelled Proj. for space. The two ratio rows are recomputed from the projected rows above them, not projected directly, and always equal the MTD figure, confirmed as intended rather than a formula to fix. Units round to whole bikes.
  • MTD tab only.
  • Two day counts, deliberate. Do not reconcile them. Header and progress bar use the accounting period, 8 of 30. The projection uses a six-day trading week, 7 of 26, which understates month-end on purpose — see Projected month-end in CLAUDE.md for the full basis.
Comparison columns replace, they don't add
  • Switching to vs. Last Month or vs. Last Year swaps each segment's Proj. column for that segment's own complete prior month figure — all of August, all of September 2025, not the same 8 days of it. Same six columns as the MTD tab, just a different second column.
  • One column is added at the very end, not three: New + Used vs. Aug 2026 (or vs. Sep 2025) carries the pace judgment for the combined total only — New and Used individually show their prior figure for reference, without a separate pace signal each, which is what keeps this table at seven columns instead of nine.
  • On that column, unit/sales/gross rows show % of prior = MTD ÷ that month's figure, coloured at ±3 points against the period elapsed. The two ratio rows show a difference instead — +$115, −0.3 pts, coloured at ±$25 / ±0.3 pts — a margin at 100% of last month is flat, not on pace.

Transactions and conversion Total transactions = parts invoices + Motorclothes invoices + service repair orders — 61 + 224 + 63 = 348 month to date.
Transactions per greet = total transactions ÷ greets, same shape for sit-downs and unit sales. Lower is better: the figure is how much traffic it takes to produce one, so the goal is a ceiling. Goals exist for the three ratios and missed opportunities only; every other row shows an em-dash, never a zero.

Per Deal

32 columns reduced to 14. One table, two date scopes.

New or used

The deal list One component, two date scopes — the Sep 08 view is the month list filtered to today, so the two can never disagree. Newest first, six rows in the frame, headings and totals pinned while the body scrolls. Any heading sorts; dates sort as dates, not as text. Totals reflect the filter, not what is on screen. In the month view today's deals are shaded with an accent rule, so the day reads apart from the month without switching to the Sep 08 view.
While collapsed the VIN shows its last 8 characters, with the full number on hover; Show all restores it. Names display as Last, F. with the full name on hover — Talon stores them in caps as LAST, FIRST, which is the widest thing in the row. Both are display conventions to keep all 14 columns visible without horizontal scrolling; either can be reverted if the full strings are wanted. Build Notes

Changes against the current tab.

What is different from the current Bike Sales page
  • New: KPI ribbon, and a sticky section menu.
  • New: the Bike Sales KPI table — fifteen scorecard tables in one, five rows with New / Used / New + Used as column groups rather than stacked blocks, with Projected month-end as a standing column on the MTD tab.
  • New: Transactions, replacing the old KPI Metrics table, and an F&I section built on the Talon deal-charge export.
  • Changed: the deal list drops from 32 columns to 14, and the two tables become one with a date scope toggle.
  • Changed: the KPI and Transactions tables lost their Day column and their default tab is now MTD, not Day w/ MTD — accurate day-of data was more complexity than the near-term scope could absorb. The comparison tabs replace the Projected column with that segment's prior-period figure rather than adding columns, with a single trailing column carrying the pace judgment for New + Used only. The Per Deal table below is the exception and keeps its Day scope, since each row there is a dated record, not a comparison basis on a metric.
  • Moved: Current Inventory, above the deal list.
  • Removed: the Sale type and Deal type selectors on the deal list. Almost every deal is in-store, and dropping them simplifies the first build.
  • Removed for now: the two percentage tables at the foot of the current page, and any cross-store ranking. Benchmarking is Phase 2 and consent-gated.

Source bands. The tag beside each row label says where the figure comes from: statement read from the Talon income statement, counted from unit or deal records, entered from the CRM, derived calculated by ERS. Derived is the only band where a formula can be wrong, and the only band needing an equation in the formula specification.
