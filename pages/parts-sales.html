Parts Summary

Every revenue channel, and the totals built from them.

Converted from the legacy Summary table
  • The old page had four period tabs (Day w/ MTD, MTD Yr-over-Yr, YTD Yr-over-Yr, MTD vs Lst MTD) and a separate Sales/Profits toggle. Replaced with the standard three tabs used everywhere else in ERS, and one table that already shows dollars, gross profit and margin together — the toggle was showing a slice of the same data a second way.
  • No Day column on this table — accurate day-of data was more complexity than the near-term scope could absorb. MTD is the default tab, not Day w/ MTD. The Counter Sales table below is the exception and keeps its Day figures, since each row there is a rep's day, not a comparison basis on a store-wide metric.
  • Cust. Pay – Service is parts sold against a customer-paid repair order — a Parts revenue channel, not Service department revenue.
Projected month-end Projected = MTD ÷ open days elapsed × total open days
  • Applied to every dollar row. No projected margin — both figures scale by the same factor, so it would equal the month-to-date margin on every row. MTD tab only.
  • Two day counts, deliberate. Do not reconcile them. Header and progress bar use the accounting period. The projection uses a six-day trading week, which understates month-end on purpose — and differs on purpose from the live page's own Projected column, which just divides by calendar days elapsed.
Comparison columns
  • Prior column is a complete month — all of August, all of September 2025 — not the same 11 days of it.
  • % of prior = MTD ÷ that month's figure, on every dollar row. Gross Margin shows a difference instead — +0.6 pts, not a percentage of last month's margin.
  • Colour against the period elapsed: green at +3 points or more, red at −3 or worse. Margin colours on the difference: ±0.3 pts.

Counter Sales

Today's channel split and month-to-date productivity, by rep.

Reduced from 17 columns to 11
  • The legacy table showed Day and MTD versions of every column side by side. Employee and Wholesale are store-wide categories that showed zero for every rep in the export and are dropped here — still on the Summary table above. Deal Add-On isn't attributed to counter staff in the source data either, so it's Summary-only too.
  • Day Sales here is Counter + Cust. Pay + Internal + Warranty for that rep, today — the categories actually tied to a person at the counter.
  • Avg Ticket and LIPT are derived (MTD counter sales ÷ tickets, and items ÷ tickets) — not stored, computed from the counts to the left.
  • Garcia, Luis and Price, Angela are sample rows, not part of the original export: the six named reps in the source data didn't add up to the Summary table's totals above, meaning more reps exist than the export showed. These two absorb the difference so this table's own totals reconcile, rather than showing a Totals row that doesn't sum.

Changes against the live page.

What's different from the current Parts Sales page
  • Changed: four period tabs and a Sales/Profits toggle collapsed into the standard three-tab pattern used on every other table in ERS.
  • Changed: the Summary table's Day column and Day w/ MTD tab are gone — MTD is the default tab now. Accurate day-of data was more complexity than the near-term scope could absorb. The Counter Sales table below keeps its Day figures, since each row there is a rep's day, not a store-wide metric.
  • Changed: the per-rep table drops Employee, Wholesale and Deal Add-On (store-wide, not rep-attributed) and the redundant Day-scoped Items/Tickets/LIPT columns, keeping the MTD versions.
  • Not built yet: Tire Sales — the live page has a heading for it but showed no data for this store or date. Whether it's a distinct revenue category or already folded into Counter Sales needs Logan before it's built, rather than guessing at a shape.
  • Removed for now: cross-store ranking on this tab. That's what Scorecards' Parts table is for.
