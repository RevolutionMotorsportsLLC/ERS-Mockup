Bike Sales KPIs

Fifteen scorecard tables in one.

Projected month-end Projected = MTD ÷ open days elapsed × total open days — here MTD ÷ 7 × 26, factor 3.71.
  • Applied to units, sales and gross. The two ratio rows are recomputed from the projected rows above them, not projected directly. Units round to whole bikes after projecting.
  • Day w/ MTD tab only.
  • Two day counts, deliberate. Do not reconcile them. Header, progress bar and comparison colour use the accounting period, 8 of 30. The projection uses a six-day trading week, 7 of 26, which understates month-end on purpose.
  • The column heading must state the projection basis — · 26 open days — so the two counts are not read as an error.
Comparison columns
  • Prior column is a complete month — all of August, all of September 2025 — not the same 8 days of it. The export only reports finished periods.
  • % of prior = MTD ÷ that month's figure, on unit, sales and gross rows.
  • Ratio rows show a difference instead — +$115, −0.3 pts. A margin at 100% of last month is flat, not on pace.
  • Colour against 26.7% elapsed: green at +3 points or more, red at −3 or worse. Ratio rows colour on the difference: ±$25, ±0.3 pts.
  • Day w/ MTD adds one column, the day's own figure. It is a value, not a comparison — never coloured.

Transactions per greet = total transactions ÷ greets, same shape for sit-downs and unit sales. Lower is better: the figure is how much traffic it takes to produce one, so the goal is a ceiling. Build Notes

Changes against the current tab.

What is different from the current Bike Sales page
  • New: KPI ribbon, and a sticky section menu.
  • New: the Bike Sales KPI table — fifteen scorecard tables in one, in New / Used / New + Used blocks, with Projected month-end as a standing column.
  • New: Transactions, replacing the old KPI Metrics table, and an F&I section built on the Talon deal-charge export.
  • Changed: the deal list drops from 32 columns to 14, and the two tables become one with a date scope toggle.
  • Changed: the four time filters add two comparison columns to the KPI table rather than switching the page. Building all four is a separate task-list line, not part of the base table.
  • Moved: Current Inventory, above the deal list.
  • Removed: the Sale type and Deal type selectors on the deal list. Almost every deal is in-store, and dropping them simplifies the first build.
  • Removed for now: the two percentage tables at the foot of the current page, and any cross-store ranking. Benchmarking is Phase 2 and consent-gated.

Source bands. The tag beside each row label says where the figure comes from: statement read from the Talon income statement, counted from unit or deal records, entered from the CRM, derived calculated by ERS. Derived is the only band where a formula can be wrong, and the only band needing an equation in the formula specification.
